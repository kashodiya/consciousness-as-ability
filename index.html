Consciousness as a Fundamental Ability

What if consciousness is not a mystery to be solved, but a fundamental ability — like energy — to be understood?

Version 0.3.0  ·  June 2026
"Consciousness is the fundamental ability to know."

Just as energy is the ability to do work, consciousness is the ability to know.
The brain is the substrate; the mind, intellect, and sense organs are the instruments through which consciousness operates.

The Core Framework
Ability

Consciousness

Universal, non-physical. The fundamental enabling condition of all knowing. Cannot be created or destroyed.

→
Capacity

Brain · Senses · Mind

Variable, physical instruments. What consciousness works through. Shapes and limits the quality of experience.

→
Consequence

Experience

The natural result of Ability working through Capacity. Not a third independent thing — a consequence.

About the Author

This theory is the work of Kaushik Ashodiya — a technologist and independent philosopher with a lifelong interest in the nature of mind and consciousness.

CoFA emerged from decades of reflection, study of classical Indian philosophy, and a desire to offer a rigorous, non-sectarian framework that bridges scientific and philosophical inquiry.

This work was written by the author and edited with the assistance of AI (Claude by Anthropic).
